### About

I build web products end to end — from internal tools and automation pipelines to client-facing sites. Most of my recent work leans on **Next.js, TypeScript, and Node.js**, with **Laravel** and **PostgreSQL** on the backend side. I care about clean UI, solid architecture, and shipping things that actually work in production, not just in a demo.

- 🔭 Currently building automation workflows and full-stack products
- 🌱 Exploring AI-assisted development and agentic tooling
- 📍 Based in the United Arab Emirates
- 💬 Ask me about Next.js, TypeScript, or system design for small teams
